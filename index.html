Our ethos

AI belongs
to everyone.

01 / OPEN TO ALL

Lehigh AI Club is an inclusive community dedicated to making AI accessible to every student, regardless of major or technical background. AI isn’t a distant technology reserved for CS majors. It’s already reshaping the way we learn, work, create, and solve problems.

02 / GO DEEPER

We want to go beyond simply using AI to ask questions or study for an exam. There’s a growing gap between what AI can actually do and how most people use it — a capability overhang. We want to help you close that gap by becoming power users: people who understand what AI can do, know how to use it effectively, and think critically about its limitations, implications, and impact on their industries.

03 / YOUR FUTURE

Whether you’re studying computer science, business, engineering, design, healthcare, the humanities, or anything in between, AI is going to be part of your future and we want you to be ready for it.
